Fajrالفجر

The dawn prayer, offered between first light and sunrise. It marks the start of the fasting day and is prayed before the sky brightens.

Dhuhrالظهر

The midday prayer, offered once the sun begins to descend from its highest point. In many cities it falls shortly before or after noon.

Asrالعصر

The afternoon prayer, offered when the shadow of an object roughly matches its length. Its window runs until shortly before sunset.

Maghribالمغرب

The sunset prayer, offered just after the sun fully sets. It has one of the shorter windows of the five prayers.

Ishaالعشاء

The night prayer, offered once twilight has faded. Its window extends until Fajr the next morning.
